Where WhoPaid is genuinely different

Day-wise splitting

This is the main reason WhoPaid exists. Most splitting apps assume a cost is shared equally by everyone in the group. That breaks the moment someone is away for half the month — a very common situation for flatmates, hostellers, and anyone sharing groceries.

Your share = (your days ÷ total person-days) × total amount

Someone present 11 days out of a 71 person-day total pays 15.5% of the bill, not 25%. You can try it on the rent split calculator.

Recurring cycles with carried-forward dues

Flatmate expenses are not one-off events; they repeat every month with the same people. WhoPaid keeps the group as a persistent roster, and anything left unpaid at the end of a month is carried into the next month's total rather than quietly forgotten.

No account, nothing on a server

Everything is stored in your browser on your own device. There is no sign-up, no email, and no expense data sitting on someone else's server. The honest trade-off is stated plainly: nothing syncs. Clear your browser data without exporting a backup first and it is gone. Settings includes a backup export for exactly this reason.

UPI settle-up

Once balances are worked out, WhoPaid generates a UPI link and QR so the person who owes can pay directly from any UPI app. No copying amounts between screens.
