WhoPaid — Free Bill Split Calculator for Trips, Rent & Group Expenses

Split any shared expense — a trip with friends, monthly rent with flatmates, a team dinner, or a group purchase. Equally, by exact amount, or day-wise. Free, no sign-up, works offline, and settles up over UPI.

What you can do

  • Split trip costs, rent, dinners, and any group expense
  • Choose equal, exact-amount, or day-wise splitting
  • Track who paid what and who owes whom
  • Settle up instantly with UPI, with transfers minimised
  • Install it like an app and use it offline — your data stays on your device

How it works

  1. Create a group and add the people sharing the cost
  2. Add each expense and mark who paid for it
  3. Split equally, by exact amount, or day-wise — WhoPaid does the maths
  4. See balances and settle up over UPI

Frequently asked questions

Is WhoPaid free to use?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up and no ads. Just open it and start splitting bills.
What kinds of expenses can I split?
Any shared expense — a trip with friends, rent and ration with flatmates, a team dinner, or a one-off group purchase.
Can I split unequally or day-wise?
Yes. Split equally, enter exact amounts per person, or split day-wise so someone present fewer days pays proportionally less.
Does WhoPaid work offline?
Yes — it's an installable PWA that works offline, with your data stored privately on your device.
How does everyone settle up?
It shows who owes whom, minimises the number of transfers, and supports UPI settle-up so you can clear balances with a tap.
